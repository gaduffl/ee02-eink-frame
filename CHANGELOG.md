# Changelog

## Docs — 2026-10-16

- Parts list with Amazon links for the US, Germany and France, generated from `parts/parts.toml`.
- Back board: notch drawing and steps. The frame's back board goes back in around the case and holds the passepartout and display; the battery lid slides out over it.
- LiPo charger added to the parts list, for charging the spare battery outside the frame.

## Rev B — 2026-10-01

Changes after the first test print:

- **Board clips:** three flexible snap clips with 45° catches replace the two fixed lips and the latch. The old holding surfaces were flat, unsupported overhangs, and the board couldn't get under the lips past the locating pins. Now the board drops straight down onto the pins.
- **Looser board fit:** 0.8 mm clearance around the board (was 0.4–0.5 mm), Ø1.9 mm pins (was 2.14 mm), and 0.25 mm of play under the catches.
- **Antenna:** a 4.4 mm slot open at the top (was a 3.4 mm hole).
- **Keys and power switch:** the power switch is side-actuated like the keys. One 45 mm opening in the upper wall now exposes both, as high as the old USB-C opening. The ON/OFF slot in the lid is gone, and the lid has key labels instead.
- **Battery:** the real cell is 60.5 × 91 × 6.5 mm with 62 mm leads at a corner. The case became L-shaped, with the battery pocket above the board under its own sliding lid, and the leads run diagonally to the JST socket.
- **USB-C:** only reachable with the battery out, because the cell now sits in front of the socket.

## Rev A — 2026-09-29

- First version: a single tray with one sliding lid, sized for a 102535 cell (10 × 25 × 35 mm), plus hangers and bumpers.
